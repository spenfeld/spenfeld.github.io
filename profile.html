BIO

I'm Spencer Feldmann, a Computer Science and Economics student at the University of Maryland with minors in Business Analytics and Rhetoric. I added the Rhetoric minor because I think the best technical work is wasted if you can't explain why it matters. That belief has shaped what I've chosen to work on: energy audit tooling as a data analyst intern at Habitat for Humanity, a full-stack development bootcamp with Hack4Impact, and now automated testing for a health claims platform at ClaimsBridge, where the stakes are people's actual healthcare. In a lot of ways, that's what I'm interested in doing long-term, bridging the gap between technology and the people it's meant to help.
